Pictures of how the driver works

Twelve short pages. Each one has buttons you press to step through a story, a line under the buttons that says what just happened, and a legend for the colours. Read them in order, or pick the one you need.

Two words used on every page

A plain volume is a folder of files, used by pods. A VM volume holds one image file per disk, called disk.img. On the pages, teal blocks are plain files and purple blocks are pieces of a VM disk.

  1. 1. Two kinds of volumeA folder of files, or one disk image per disk: how snapshots, backups and replication treat each.
  2. 2. Pool, share and volumeHow a pool of file shares holds many volumes as folders.
  3. 3. Replication to another clusterKeep a copy of your volumes in another cluster, sent on a schedule to a receiver.
  4. 4. Failover and failbackStart your work in the other cluster after a loss, and move back later.
  5. 5. Backup to COSSend locked, encrypted backups to an object storage bucket, and restore from it.
  6. 6. The backup key chainThe keys that protect a backup, and which one you need to get it back.
  7. 7. Cloning a VM diskWhy a clone takes time, and why the VM has to wait for it.
  8. 8. How a pod gets its volumeThe steps from a claim in Kubernetes to a folder inside the pod.
  9. 9. A pool filling upWhen a pool warns you, when it adds a share, and what the percent numbers mean.
  10. 10. Several disks, one momentHow all the disks of one VM are copied at the same moment.
  11. 11. What QEMU doesThe program that runs each VM: how it copies a running disk at one moment, and why a move starts a full copy.
  12. 12. Instant VM snapshotsSnapshots that copy nothing: the chain of files, the two settings that turn them on, and what happens when the VM restarts.

The pages are teaching pictures with small, made-up sizes. The real rules are in the documents in the docs folder, for example CONTEXT.md. If a page and the documents disagree, the documents are right: please report it.
